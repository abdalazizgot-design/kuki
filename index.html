Spaghetti Carbonara

⏱ 25 min · 👨‍🍳 Easy · 🍽 2 servings

A creamy, classic pasta made with eggs, cheese, pepper and crispy pancetta.

Ingredients

🍝 Spaghetti 200 g
🥓 Guanciale 100 g
🥚 Eggs 2
🧀 Parmesan 50 g
🌶 Black pepper 1 tsp
🧂 Salt 1 tbsp
Step 1 of 7
Boil the pasta
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook for 8–10 minutes, until al dente.
